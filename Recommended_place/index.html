Three of the best places to hang out or spend time with friends or someone special.

The Places

Cafe I Am Here!

I recommend this café because it is a comfortable place to relax alone or with someone. It has private booths, pillows, good food and beverages, sticky notepads, air conditioning, and a nice window view.

Intramuros

I recommend Intramuros because it is a peaceful and beautiful place where you can relax, explore historical sites, and learn more about Philippine history and culture.

Arroceros Forest Park

I recommend Arroceros because it is a peaceful and refreshing place in the middle of the city. It is a great place to walk, relax, enjoy nature, and take a break from the busy surroundings.
