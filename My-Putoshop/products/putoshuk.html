About Putoshuk

Putoshuk is a soft and fluffy Filipino steamed rice cake infused with the fragrant flavor of pandan. It is made with rice flour, all-purpose flour, sugar, baking powder, coconut milk, evaporated milk, eggs, pandan extract, and a small amount of salt. These ingredients are carefully combined to create a soft, moist, and slightly sweet puto with a distinct pandan aroma.

The mixture is steamed until the puto becomes light and fluffy. Each piece is prepared with care and dedication to give customers a flavorful Filipino treat that can be enjoyed as a snack or dessert.
