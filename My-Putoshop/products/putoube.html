About Puto Ube

Puto Ube is a soft and fluffy Filipino puto infused with the rich and sweet flavor of ube. Its purple color and delicious taste give the traditional Filipino puto an exciting twist.

Each piece is carefully prepared and steamed until soft and fluffy, making it a delicious Filipino snack or dessert.

Ingredients

  • Rice flour
  • All-purpose flour
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Coconut milk
  • Evaporated milk
  • Eggs
  • Ube flavoring
  • Salt
  • Secret Ingredient
