About Putoshuk

Putoshuk started from a simple moment during my college days. One day, while eating a cheap piece of puto and counting the little money I had left, I realized how difficult it was to get a decent meal while trying to survive on a limited budget. Out of nowhere, the idea came to me: why not create a shop focused on puto and its different varieties? What started as a random thought slowly became the inspiration behind Putoshuk.

Putoshuk is more than just a shop. Every product is made with love, dedication, and hard work because it represents my journey as a college student working toward graduation and a better future. I want every piece of puto to feel special—not only because of its flavor and variety, but because it carries the effort and determination behind it. Through Putoshuk, I hope to make a living, continue my education, and prove that even a simple idea can become something meaningful when it is made with passion and perseverance.
